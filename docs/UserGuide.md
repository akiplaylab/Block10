# User guide

[English](UserGuide.md) | [日本語](UserGuide.ja.md)

## How to play

[Open Block10](https://akiplaylab.github.io/block10/) and press **▶** to start with 10. Choose a favorite number card to play with that number, or press the **shuffle icon** to play with the numbers shown on its card.

Find the number that goes in the **?**. In `7 + ? = 10`, work out how many to add to 7 to make 10. In `10 − ? = 7`, work out how many to take away from 10 to leave 7.

- The **blocks and numbers** preview lets you pick how many blocks to add or take away.
- The **row of numbers** preview lets you choose the answer.
- The **number pad** preview lets you enter the answer using the number buttons. A correct answer takes you straight to the next problem.

If you make a mistake, you can try the same problem again. With blocks, the result stays on screen until you answer again. When adding, look for empty spaces or extra blocks below the frame. When subtracting, look for orange blocks left behind or gaps where blue blocks were taken away.

A **check mark with stars** shows a correct answer. Press **→** when it appears to move on. Press the **house icon** to return home.

## Settings

Press the gear button to open **Settings** and **×** to close it. On the home screen, choose **+** or **−** under **Math**. You can change **How to answer** and tap the **speaker icon** to toggle sound while playing.

Next to the **globe icon**, choose **English** or **日本語**. Your choice takes effect right away and is remembered next time. On your first visit, Block10 uses Japanese if your browser language is Japanese and English otherwise.

To change a favorite number or the shuffle numbers, press the edit button on its card on the home screen. Choose one to three shuffle numbers and press **×** to close. Closing with no numbers selected restores 5 and 10.

Choose **Reset settings** from **Settings** on the home screen to restore the game settings. Your play history and choice of language are kept.

## Installation and offline play

- On iPad or iPhone, open the Share menu in Safari and choose **Add to Home Screen**. If **Open as Web App** appears, turn it on.
- On Windows, choose **Apps** and then **Install this site as an app** in Microsoft Edge.

To play offline, open Block10 once while connected to the internet. When **You can play Block10 offline** appears in **Settings**, you can play in either language without an internet connection.
